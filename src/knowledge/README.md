# Knowledge Layer

## Purpose
Store governed, versioned business knowledge that gives intelligence and Digital Employees reliable context.

## Responsibilities
Manage facts, policies, definitions, sources, confidence, freshness, approvals, and conflict resolution.

## Inputs
Evidence, approved research findings, user decisions, policy documents, outcomes, and provider provenance.

## Outputs
Retrievable knowledge records, citations, policy decisions, change events, and invalidation notices.

## Future APIs
publish, retrieve, search, approve, invalidate, and explainSource.

## Dependencies
Shared types, memory ports, event contracts, and future storage or retrieval adapters.
