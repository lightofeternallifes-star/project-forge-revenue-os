# Digital Employees

## Purpose
Define bounded Digital Employee roles that may later operate through the core runtime.

## Responsibilities
Specify role purpose, allowed capabilities, inputs, outputs, approval requirements, escalation rules, and measurable outcomes.

## Inputs
Typed tasks, governed context, policies, tool capabilities, and human approvals.

## Outputs
Recommendations, drafts, structured actions, escalations, and audit records. No role implies autonomous execution at this stage.

## Future APIs
EmployeeRegistry, assignTask, requestApproval, executeApprovedAction, and recordOutcome.

## Dependencies
Core orchestrator, workflows, memory, events, knowledge, intelligence, and provider-neutral tools.
