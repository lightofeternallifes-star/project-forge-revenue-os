# Titan

## Purpose
Define the future CRM and revenue operations Digital Employee.

## Responsibilities
Maintain accountable records, identify state inconsistencies, prepare handoffs, and surface operational exceptions.

## Inputs
Workflow events, CRM records, appointment outcomes, ownership policies, and human decisions.

## Outputs
CRM change proposals, task and handoff recommendations, data-quality findings, and escalations.

## Future APIs
reconcileRecord, prepareHandoff, and identifyException.

## Dependencies
CRM intelligence, events, memory, knowledge policy, and provider-neutral CRM ports.
