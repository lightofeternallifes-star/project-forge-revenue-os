# Atlas

## Purpose
Define the future Prospect and Company Intelligence Digital Employee.

## Responsibilities
Research accounts and contacts, assemble attributable context, identify missing information, and surface qualification signals.

## Inputs
Research tasks, ICP context, company and contact records, approved provider capabilities, and policies.

## Outputs
Research briefs, enrichment proposals, confidence, citations, and escalation requests.

## Future APIs
researchAccount, researchContact, and identifyGaps.

## Dependencies
Research, companies, contacts, enrichment, memory, and core workflow contracts.
