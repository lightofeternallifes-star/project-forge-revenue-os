# Echo

## Purpose
Define the future Conversation and Customer Support Digital Employee.

## Responsibilities
Classify conversation intent, preserve context, propose responses, identify urgency, and escalate when boundaries are reached.

## Inputs
Conversation events, contact and company context, policies, channel constraints, and human feedback.

## Outputs
Intent records, response drafts, next-action proposals, escalation requests, and outcome signals.

## Future APIs
classifyIntent, draftResponse, and escalateConversation.

## Dependencies
Core events, memory, intelligence, knowledge policy, model-neutral inference, and future channel ports.
