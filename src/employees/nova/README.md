# Nova

## Purpose
Define the future Outreach Intelligence and personalization Digital Employee.

## Responsibilities
Recommend channel and message strategy from approved context, identify unsupported claims, and prepare human-reviewable drafts.

## Inputs
ICP context, research briefs, brand policy, channel constraints, and conversation history.

## Outputs
Outreach plans, grounded draft proposals, rationale, risk flags, and approval requests.

## Future APIs
planOutreach, draftMessage, and reviewGrounding.

## Dependencies
Research, ICP, knowledge, memory, model-neutral inference, and future outreach contracts.
