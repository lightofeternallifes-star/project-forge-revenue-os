# Intelligence

## Purpose
Turn external observations and internal context into explainable revenue intelligence.

## Responsibilities
Own company, contact, ICP, scoring, enrichment, and research concepts without provider transport or outreach execution.

## Inputs
Normalized provider observations, knowledge, memory, criteria, and outcome signals.

## Outputs
Versioned intelligence records, explainable scores, research briefs, enrichment proposals, and confidence metadata.

## Future APIs
Domain services for company profile, contact profile, ICP definition, score, enrichment plan, and research brief.

## Dependencies
Shared types, core memory and event ports, knowledge policy, and provider-neutral interfaces.
