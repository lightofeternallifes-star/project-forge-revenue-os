# Enrichment Intelligence

## Purpose
Plan and govern the improvement of company and contact records from approved sources.

## Responsibilities
Define enrichment requests, field confidence, source priority, conflict resolution, freshness, and human review.

## Inputs
Existing profiles, missing-field policies, provider capabilities, consent constraints, and research priorities.

## Outputs
Enrichment plans, proposed field updates, provenance, confidence, and review events.

## Future APIs
plan, proposeUpdates, resolveConflict, and refresh.

## Dependencies
Companies, contacts, provider-neutral enrichment ports, knowledge policy, memory, and events.
