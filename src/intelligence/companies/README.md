# Companies Intelligence

## Purpose
Maintain normalized, attributable company intelligence for revenue decisions.

## Responsibilities
Resolve company identity, model firmographic and strategic attributes, track freshness, and expose evidence-backed context.

## Inputs
Provider observations, domain identifiers, research findings, enrichment results, and user corrections.

## Outputs
Company profiles, identity matches, attribute observations, confidence, provenance, and change events.

## Future APIs
getCompany, resolveCompany, recordObservation, and getTimeline.

## Dependencies
Shared types, memory, events, and provider-neutral enrichment ports.
