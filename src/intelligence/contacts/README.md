# Contacts Intelligence

## Purpose
Maintain normalized contact and persona intelligence connected to companies and revenue context.

## Responsibilities
Resolve identity, model role and buying context, track data quality, and expose attributable observations.

## Inputs
Provider observations, company links, research findings, enrichment results, consent metadata, and corrections.

## Outputs
Contact profiles, identity matches, persona observations, confidence, provenance, and change events.

## Future APIs
getContact, resolveContact, recordObservation, and getRelationshipContext.

## Dependencies
Shared types, companies intelligence, memory, events, and provider-neutral ports.
