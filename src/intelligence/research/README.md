# Sales Research Intelligence

## Purpose
Produce structured, attributable research briefs that improve revenue decisions.

## Responsibilities
Define research questions, collect approved observations, cite sources, distinguish facts from inference, and track freshness.

## Inputs
Company and contact context, research questions, approved provider capabilities, and knowledge policies.

## Outputs
Research briefs, source references, findings, confidence, open questions, and completion events.

## Future APIs
createBrief, addFinding, assessSource, and publishBrief.

## Dependencies
Companies, contacts, knowledge, memory, events, and provider-neutral research or model ports.
