# ICP Intelligence

## Purpose
Represent and evaluate ideal customer profile definitions as explicit, versioned business hypotheses.

## Responsibilities
Model criteria, exclusions, buying signals, segments, evidence, versioning, and approval state.

## Inputs
Company and contact intelligence, research findings, outcome signals, and user-defined criteria.

## Outputs
ICP definitions, match explanations, segment membership proposals, confidence, and revision events.

## Future APIs
createICP, evaluateMatch, versionICP, and explainCriteria.

## Dependencies
Companies, contacts, scoring, knowledge, memory, and events.
