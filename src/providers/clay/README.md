# Clay Provider

## Purpose
Reserve an adapter boundary for Clay research and enrichment capabilities.

## Responsibilities
Translate approved requests, normalize results, preserve source lineage, and isolate provider limits.

## Inputs
Research or enrichment requests and future managed credentials.

## Outputs
Normalized observations, provenance, capability metadata, and typed errors.

## Future APIs
Enrichment and research adapters behind stable ports.

## Dependencies
Provider contracts and shared types only.
