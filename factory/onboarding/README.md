# Onboarding Module

## Purpose

Open an approved employee record and establish identity, employment, permissions, knowledge, and organizational assignment.

## Responsibilities

Create the onboarding checklist, collect approvals, issue identity records, bind manager and department ownership, and establish initial access boundaries.

## Inputs

Approved Employee Definition, requisition approval, contract policy, NDA policy, organization chart, and brand references.

## Outputs

Onboarding record, employee profile, contract, NDA, passport, badge, ID, signature, knowledge profile, competency baseline, and assignment.

## Future APIs

`openOnboarding(employeeId)`, `completeOnboardingStage(employeeId, evidence)`, `issueIdentityPackage(employeeId)`.

## Dependencies

Generators, Identity, Organization, Security, Compliance, Knowledge, and Audit.
