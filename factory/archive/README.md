# Archive Module

## Purpose

Preserve employee history and control retirement, suspension, retention, legal hold, and capability revocation.

## Responsibilities

Seal artifact manifests, record disposition, revoke capabilities, retain evidence, and prevent destructive edits to historical records.

## Inputs

Employee record set, retirement or suspension decision, retention policy, legal holds, capability inventory, and final timeline.

## Outputs

Archive manifest, sealed package, disposition decision, revocation record, and audit event.

## Future APIs

`prepareArchive(employeeId)`, `retireEmployee(employeeId)`, `restoreArchivedRecord(recordId)`.

## Dependencies

Lifecycle, Registry, Security, Compliance, Audit, Evidence, and Artifact Store.
