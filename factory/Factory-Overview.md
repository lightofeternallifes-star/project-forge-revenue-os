# Factory Overview

The factory creates a complete Digital Employee package from a single approved input. It is reusable across departments and specializations and supports both human approvals and future controlled automation.

## Inputs

Employee number, employee name, division, department, manager, role, specializations, mission assignment, certification requirements, approved brand references, permission profile, dates, and responsible approvers.

## Outputs

Identity, employment, training, mission, performance, evidence, certification, graduation, promotion, registry, organizational assignment, knowledge, recognition, communications, and archive records.

## Non-Goals

The factory does not create logos, invent employee capabilities, assign unapproved permissions, fabricate evidence, or alter historical records.
