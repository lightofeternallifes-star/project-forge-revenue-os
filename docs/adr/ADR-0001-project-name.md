# ADR-0001: Rename Growth Engine To Revenue OS

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Decision owners:** PROJECT FORGE

## Context

The former project identity, centered on a Growth Engine, described an important initial emphasis on lead generation and outbound growth. The intended platform scope has expanded to include Prospect Intelligence, Company Intelligence, an ICP Engine, Sales Research, Outreach Intelligence, Email and LinkedIn Engines, Voice AI, WhatsApp AI, CRM Intelligence, Appointment Scheduling, Digital Employees, Revenue Analytics, and Continuous Learning.

The expanded scope covers the connected commercial lifecycle rather than one growth function. A name centered on growth risks narrowing product decisions, architecture boundaries, and future user expectations around acquisition alone.

## Decision

Rename the project identity to **PROJECT FORGE Revenue OS™** and use it consistently across repository documentation, mission records, architecture notes, roadmaps, templates, and reports.

## Rationale

“Revenue OS” better represents a platform that coordinates intelligence, engagement, conversion, support, measurement, and learning. It communicates an operating layer for the revenue organization, while leaving room for multiple channels, CRM workflows, human collaboration, and specialized Digital Employees.

The name also supports a broader architecture: shared commercial context, governed AI decisions, workflow orchestration, channel adapters, scheduling, analytics, and continuous learning can be treated as connected platform capabilities rather than isolated growth features.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Product discovery can cover the full revenue lifecycle.
- Architecture and knowledge organization can treat intelligence, actions, and outcomes as connected concerns.
- The identity better supports sales, marketing, customer success, and Digital Employee workflows.
- Roadmap conversations can prioritize durable operating capabilities over channel-specific feature accumulation.

### Tradeoffs

- The broader name raises expectations for CRM, support, governance, analytics, and reliability.
- Existing growth-focused research must be reframed within the larger commercial system.
- Future scope needs explicit boundaries to avoid becoming an undifferentiated all-in-one product.

## Rejected Alternatives

- **Keep Growth Engine:** too narrow for the stated long-term platform scope.
- **Revenue Automation:** emphasizes execution but underrepresents intelligence, analytics, support, and learning.
- **AI Sales Platform:** excludes non-sales revenue workflows and over-centers a single technology category.

## Follow-Up

- Update all future mission briefs and reports to use PROJECT FORGE Revenue OS™.
- Maintain evidence-backed boundaries for each capability area.
- Revisit the name only through a subsequent ADR if market research demonstrates material confusion or strategic mismatch.
