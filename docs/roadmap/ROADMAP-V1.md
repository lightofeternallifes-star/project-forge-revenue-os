# PROJECT FORGE Revenue OS™ Roadmap V1

## Purpose

Sequence implementation from stable contracts to measurable revenue capabilities. Each phase requires evidence, contract tests, security review, and explicit exit criteria before the next phase expands scope.

## Phase 1: Competitive Intelligence

Define company, contact, research, provenance, and knowledge contracts. Exit when provider-neutral domain schemas and evidence-backed research workflows are reviewed.

## Phase 2: Provider Integrations

Implement optional adapters behind capability ports, beginning with one provider per capability. Exit when adapters have contract tests, error translation, health signals, and no provider leakage into domain modules.

## Phase 3: Digital Employees

Implement bounded employee task contracts, approvals, context scopes, escalation, and audit records. Exit when one narrow role can operate with human review and measurable reliability.

## Phase 4: Revenue Intelligence

Connect company, contact, ICP, scoring, enrichment, and research capabilities into explainable intelligence workflows. Exit when users can inspect evidence, confidence, freshness, and decision rationale.

## Phase 5: Outreach Engine

Add grounded outreach planning, channel policy, approvals, suppression, delivery state, and outcome capture. Exit when no action can bypass consent, approval, or stop rules.

## Phase 6: CRM Intelligence

Add provider-neutral CRM records, mappings, reconciliation, ownership, handoffs, and change proposals. Exit when synchronization is auditable and conflict-safe.

## Phase 7: Analytics

Define event-derived revenue metrics, attribution, funnel states, quality measures, freshness, and anomaly signals. Exit when metrics are traceable to source events and support operational decisions.

## Phase 8: Continuous Learning

Use approved outcomes and feedback to improve ICP, scoring, research, outreach, and employee policies. Exit when changes are versioned, evaluated, reversible, and attributable to evidence.

## Cross-Phase Gates

- Provider neutrality and contract tests
- Tenant isolation and privacy review
- Provenance, confidence, and freshness on intelligence
- Human approval for consequential actions
- Idempotency, retries, cancellation, and auditability
- Clear rollback and data-retention behavior
