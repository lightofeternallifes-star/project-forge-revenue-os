# Communication Protocol

## Canonical Communication

Departments communicate through Digital Brain entities, versioned commands, events, Tasks, Knowledge Records, Evidence Records, Notifications, and Audit Records. Chat, email, or meetings may coordinate humans but are not the system of record until captured through a canonical contract.

## Command Authority

- Executives issue strategic directives and approve enterprise policy, budget, and risk decisions.
- Directors issue departmental priorities and approve department-owned policies.
- Managers assign bounded work and approve routine operational decisions within policy.
- Digital Employees issue recommendations or approved capability calls only within their grants.
- Specialists and Workers provide evidence, perform assigned tasks, and escalate exceptions.

## Communication Requirements

Every material communication identifies sender, recipient, organization, subject entity, purpose, urgency, requested decision, evidence, due time, and confidentiality. Commands require an owner and acceptance. Events are facts and cannot be used as implicit authorization.

## Cross-Department Handoffs

Handoffs include current state, requested outcome, source and evidence references, open risks, owner, due time, policy version, and acceptance or rejection. Rejected handoffs return a reason and next escalation path.
