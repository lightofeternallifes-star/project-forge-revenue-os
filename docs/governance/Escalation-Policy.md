# Escalation Policy

## Severity

- **P0:** Immediate risk to safety, security, legality, customer trust, financial integrity, or platform availability. Stop affected actions and notify the executive owner, Security or Compliance, and Audit.
- **P1:** Material customer, revenue, data-quality, or operational impact requiring same-day owner response.
- **P2:** Bounded issue with workaround; Manager resolves within service level.
- **P3:** Improvement, ambiguity, or low-impact defect; capture in department backlog.

## Escalation Path

Worker or Specialist → Manager → Director → Executive owner → CEO or relevant Council. Security, Compliance, and Audit may bypass the line for P0 or control violations.

## Digital Employee Rules

Digital Employees stop and escalate when permission is absent, evidence is insufficient, confidence is below policy, a requested action is irreversible, a user disputes a record, a safety or compliance boundary is reached, or a provider route violates policy. Employees cannot lower severity, close their own incident, or approve their own exception.

## Required Escalation Record

Record issue, affected entities, severity, evidence, current action, stop decision, owner, deadline, requested decision, policy version, and resolution. Closure requires owner confirmation and audit evidence.
