# Decision Matrix

| Decision | Routine owner | Approval required | Blocking reviewers | Final escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entity correction | Owning department Manager | Director for identity or sensitive fields | Compliance when personal or regulated | Executive owner |
| Qualification decision | Lead Qualification Manager | Per policy or human review threshold | Evidence and Compliance as applicable | CRO |
| Proposal approval | Proposal Director | Commercial and delegated financial approval | Finance, Compliance | CRO or CFO |
| Customer risk response | Customer Success Manager | Executive for material churn or safety risk | Security or Compliance as applicable | CRO or CEO |
| Digital Employee capability | Employee Operations Director | Digital Employee Council | Security, Compliance, Platform | Chief Digital Employee Officer |
| Provider route or fallback | Provider Management Manager | Policy-based; executive for strategic change | Security, Finance, Platform | CTO |
| Schema or event breaking change | Platform Engineering Director | Architecture Council | All consuming owners | CTO |
| Data export or cross-tenant share | Data owner Manager | Explicit authorized approval | Security, Compliance, Audit | Chief Risk and Trust Officer |
| Learning policy publication | Learning Director | Evaluation and owner approval | Security, Compliance, Employee Operations | Intelligence Council |
| Audit finding closure | Finding owner | Auditor verification | Audit | Chief Risk and Trust Officer |
