# Governance Model

## Governance Bodies

- **Executive Council:** strategy, investment, customer promise, and cross-department conflicts.
- **Revenue Council:** pipeline, customer relationships, proposals, CRM, and revenue performance.
- **Intelligence Council:** data quality, evidence, knowledge, research, scoring, and learning.
- **Digital Employee Council:** role safety, capability grants, evaluation, costs, and retirement.
- **Trust Council:** security, compliance, audit, privacy, residency, and incident response.
- **Architecture Council:** contracts, versioning, scalability, provider neutrality, and migration.

## Decision Rights

The accountable department owns ordinary decisions within approved policy. Cross-domain changes require affected-owner review. Security, Compliance, and Audit can block work within their control boundaries. The CEO or delegated executive resolves conflicts that affect strategy, customer trust, capital, or risk appetite.

## Controls

All policies have owner, scope, version, effective date, evidence basis, approval, review date, and rollback or supersession path. Every Digital Employee capability grant, provider route, data export, permission change, and consequential action is auditable.
