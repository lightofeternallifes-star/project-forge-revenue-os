# Reporting Structure

## Formal Reporting

The CEO is the final accountable executive. Executive leaders report to the CEO. Department Directors report to the executive leader shown in the organizational hierarchy. Managers report to their department Director. Digital Employees report operationally to a Manager and functionally to their department Director. Specialists and Workers report to Managers.

## Executive Ownership

| Executive | Departments | Primary accountability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chief Revenue Officer | Revenue Operations, Sales Intelligence, Company Intelligence, Contact Intelligence, Research, Marketing Intelligence, Voice AI, WhatsApp AI, CRM Intelligence, Proposal Intelligence, Customer Success | Revenue and customer relationship outcomes |
| Chief Knowledge and Intelligence Officer | Knowledge, Atlas Intelligence, Learning | Enterprise knowledge and intelligence quality |
| Chief Digital Employee Officer | Digital Employee Operations, Automation | Employee fleet safety, capacity, and execution governance |
| Chief Technology Officer | Infrastructure, Platform Engineering, Provider Management, Analytics | Platform reliability, contracts, providers, and measurement |
| Chief Risk and Trust Officer | Security, Compliance, Audit | Security, compliance, and independent assurance |
| Chief Financial Officer | Finance Intelligence | Financial truth, investment, cost, and planning |

## Matrix Reporting

Functional review may cross executive lines without changing accountability. Security, Compliance, and Audit may block or escalate work. Analytics may certify metric definitions but does not own source entities. Knowledge may reject unpublished or unsupported shared context but does not own the source domain.
