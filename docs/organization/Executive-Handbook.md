# Executive Handbook

## Executive Operating Principles

1. Own outcomes, not activity volume.
2. Preserve one accountable owner for every domain.
3. Require evidence and confidence before strategic claims.
4. Treat customer relationships as human-accountable assets.
5. Scale by contracts, partitions, and policy, not informal coordination.
6. Never trade away security, consent, auditability, or reversibility for speed.

## Executive Review Pack

Every executive review includes revenue performance, customer health, intelligence quality, Digital Employee performance, provider health, security and compliance risk, financial variance, learning results, open escalations, and decisions required.

## Executive Questions

- What changed, and what evidence supports it?
- Who owns the next decision?
- Which customers, companies, or users are affected?
- What is automated, what requires approval, and what is reversible?
- What did the organization learn, and how is that learning governed?
