# Corporate Employee Lifecycle Standard

## Status

**Standard:** PROJECT FORGE Corporate Employee Lifecycle™
**Gold standard:** DE-012A ATLAS ANALYST™
**Employer:** Carriersfy AI
**Effective date:** 2026-07-18
**Owner:** Digital Employee Operations

## Lifecycle

Every Digital Employee passes through the same controlled stages:

1. **Requisition:** business need, role scope, accountable department, budget, and risk class are approved.
2. **Identity:** employee ID, corporate passport, badge, personnel file, portrait, signature, contract, NDA, email, and organizational assignment are issued.
3. **Training:** learning record, courses, knowledge domains, skills matrix, competency matrix, and supervised missions are recorded.
4. **Probation:** the employee performs bounded missions under review with no unapproved autonomy.
5. **Certification:** evidence, evaluation results, KPIs, permissions, mission outcomes, and executive review are assessed.
6. **Graduation:** certificate and diploma are issued; the employee enters the Corporate Registry and Employee Database.
7. **Promotion:** scope, title, department, permissions, knowledge ownership, and compensation-equivalent authority are reviewed and versioned.
8. **Active service:** missions, performance, knowledge, evidence, audits, and continuous learning are maintained.
9. **Restriction or suspension:** access and missions pause when risk, policy, evidence, or performance thresholds fail.
10. **Retirement:** capabilities are revoked, memory and records are retained or deleted by policy, and an archive record is sealed.

## Required Permanent Records

Identity, employment, training, probation, performance, operations, knowledge, permissions, evidence, audit trail, promotion history, and archive disposition are mandatory. Missing information is recorded as an explicit reviewed status, never silently omitted.

## Approval Gates

Requisition requires department and executive approval. Training completion requires Manager review. Certification requires independent evidence review. Graduation requires Executive Office approval. Promotion requires department owner, Digital Employee Operations, and Audit acknowledgement. Retirement requires capability revocation and archive confirmation.

## Gold-Standard Inheritance

Every future Digital Employee inherits DE-012A’s record categories, evidence obligations, mission-history format, certification gate, promotion record, and permanent registry pattern. A future employee may add domain-specific records but may not remove the baseline lifecycle.

## Brand Compliance

All employee records use the canonical Carriersfy AI branding policy in `branding/canonical/`. Documents reference the official corporate logo and approved brand values from the canonical source of truth. No employee package may redraw, replace, duplicate, or invent corporate branding assets.

## Control Rules

- A Digital Employee cannot approve its own training, promotion, exception, or retirement.
- No mission is counted as successful without an outcome, evidence references, reviewer, and status.
- No permission is implied by title; permission is granted by policy and recorded in the Audit Engine.
- Corporate records are provider agnostic and remain valid if models, tools, or vendors change.
- Historical records are append-only. Corrections create amendments with reason, author, date, and evidence.
