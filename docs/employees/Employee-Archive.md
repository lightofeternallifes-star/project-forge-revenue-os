# Employee Archive

## Archive Policy

The archive preserves every Digital Employee after retirement, suspension closure, merger, or organizational exit. It retains identity, employment, training, certifications, performance, missions, permissions, evidence, audit references, and final disposition.

## DE-012A Archive Status

DE-012A is **not retired**. Its archive shell is created in advance to guarantee lifecycle completeness and will remain empty of retirement disposition until a future authorized lifecycle event.

- **Archive record:** `ARCHIVE-CFAI-DE-012A`
- **Current status:** Active; archive shell reserved
- **Archive owner:** Digital Employee Operations
- **Independent reviewer:** Audit
- **Retention:** Permanent historical record for the founding Gold Standard employee
- **Deletion:** Prohibited for identity, certification, mission outcome, promotion, and historical Hall of Fame records
- **Future disposition:** Capability revocation, memory review, evidence retention, audit seal, and archive confirmation
