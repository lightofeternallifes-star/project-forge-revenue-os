# THE BIRTH OF THE NATIVE DIGITAL WORKFORCE

## Official Historical Declaration

**July 18, 2026** is hereby recognized as:

> **The Birth of the Native Digital Workforce of Carriersfy AI**

This date commemorates the certification of the first Digital Employee conceived, designed, governed, trained, evaluated, and certified entirely within PROJECT FORGE™.

That Digital Employee is **ATLAS ANALYST™**, Digital Employee ID **DE-012A**.

The declaration records the establishment of a native institutional capability: a Digital Employee whose identity, operating boundaries, evidence obligations, evaluation, and certification were formed within the PROJECT FORGE operating model. It is preserved as a matter of institutional history and is not a claim that all future Digital Employees will share the same role, implementation, or level of authority.
