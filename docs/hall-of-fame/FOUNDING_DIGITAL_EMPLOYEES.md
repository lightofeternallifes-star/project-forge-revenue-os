# FOUNDING DIGITAL EMPLOYEES

The founding generation established the first two certification paths associated with the Carriersfy AI Digital Workforce: external certification through Google's Gemini ecosystem and native certification within PROJECT FORGE™.

## Entry 1 — 🥇 MAX

**Status:** Founding External Certified Digital Employee  
**Certification:** Google Gemini  
**Contribution:** First Digital Employee externally certified through Google's Gemini ecosystem. MAX demonstrated that Digital Employee certification was possible.

MAX is preserved in this archive as the pioneer of the external certification path.

## Entry 2 — 🥈 ATLAS ANALYST™

**Digital Employee ID:** `DE-012A`  
**Status:** Founding Native Certified Digital Employee  
**Certification:** PROJECT FORGE™  
**Contribution:** First Digital Employee conceived, designed, governed, trained, evaluated, and certified entirely inside PROJECT FORGE™. Atlas Analyst established the operational standard for all future PROJECT FORGE Digital Employees.

Atlas Analyst is preserved in this archive as the pioneer of native PROJECT FORGE certification. Its formal historical profile is recorded in [DE-012A — Atlas Analyst™](DE-012A-ATLAS-ANALYST.md).

## Founding Distinction

MAX pioneered external certification. ATLAS ANALYST™ pioneered native PROJECT FORGE certification. Together they became the founding members of the Carriersfy AI Digital Workforce.

This founding record is based on the Mission 009 certification record and, for Atlas Analyst's role and operating purpose, the PROJECT FORGE Digital Employee definition in [`src/employees/atlas/README.md`](../../src/employees/atlas/README.md).
